🌸 Week of August 31, 2026

I got a Spark!

📖 Scripture

“Ask, and it shall be given you; seek, and ye shall find; knock, and it shall be opened unto you.”

Matthew 7:7 (KJV)

💜 Weekly Encouragement

For those of you who know me, you know that I am on a leap of faith journey with Jesus Christ. For those of you who do not, now you do! I have been on this journey for an entire year now. I have been trusting Jesus to supply all of my needs since I said Yes and quit my job last August. He has provided for me in ways that I never expected. Some of the ways included monetary items, but most of them have not. This journey became very difficult over the past six months. In fact, it became harder than I thought it would ever be. With the grace, support, love and patience of Jesus, I have endured. Jesus is refining me. He is helping me lose my old identity and step into the new identity I have in Him. This process is not easy. In fact, it is unbearable at times. However, when I feel like I cannot bear it any longer, He gives me the strength I need to keep stepping. I continue to step in faith daily, taking one step at a time. I am sharing this because I feel someone needs to hear this. Recently, this process became even harder when our electricity was disconnected and Duke energy refused to work with me. Despite all of my efforts to sell our belongings and collect the amount to pay them, they closed my account with no explanation. I took this closure as a sign from Jesus that I was not meant to repair this account. This was a hard “pill to swallow” and I sat down and admitted defeat. I told Jesus that I would do whatever He wanted me to do. If that meant staying at my daughter’s, then we would stay. But, I also asked Him for another way. Staying at my daughter’s is not comfortable for me. I really want to sleep in my own bed! So, on Friday night I just admitted defeat and asked Jesus to help me get to sleep in my own bed. I prayed that simple prayer and then I let it go. I knew there was nothing else I could do on my own. I choose joy and will find joy in whatever is provided to me. Today, an angel in disguise turned my electricity back on! This person was someone that I least expected. In fact, someone that I felt had been unemotional toward me. When I thought they were calling to give me grief, instead turned out to be one of the biggest blessings I have needed! And they were very kind and sympathetic and even told me to keep my chin up. It was so unexpected and unbelievable! There is no doubt in my mind that this was God! God heard my sincere prayer and gave me this blessing! He knew this journey was becoming too difficult to bear and He gave me the greatest hope to keep going! I am sharing this with you in the hopes to shed light on who God/Jesus is. He is our savior. He is our friend. He is our redeemer. His love never ends. His patience is efortless. His wisdom cannot compare. His strength is un breakable. He is always there. He will never leave you. He will never turn away. He can be fully trusted. He will guide you every day. He loves you abundantly. He wants you to succeed. He will never leave or forsake you. He will provide exactly what you need. In Matthew 7:7 Jesus says “Ask, and it shall be given you; seek, and ye shall find; knock, and it shall be opened unto you.” My testimony today is your reminder that no matter what you are praying for, if you continue to ask, seek and knock… it shall be given to you! It is not always given to you the way you think it will be. Just as my prayer was not answered in the way I thought it would be. But, my prayer was heard and answered at just the right time. Jesus loves you abundantly and wants to give you what you ask for. Keep asking. Keep believing. Keep stepping obediently. When the time is right, it will be answered and it will be in a way that you never imagined. I love you no matter what! Jesus loves you even more! Amber God’s Yes Girl 8/31/26

I got a Spark!

Ask and it shall be given to you. I encourage you to continue to ask. If you believe that what you are asking for will benefit you to help the Kingdom of God, then it will be given to you. My testimony is proof that God answers our prays in unique and unexpected ways!

I am very grateful, thankful and truly blessed today and every day. In your Heavenly name I pray, Amen.
