🌸 Week of August 10, 2026

Blessed is the one who trusts in the Lord

📖 Scripture

“But blessed is the one who trusts in the Lord, whose confidence is in him. They will be like a tree planted by the water that sends out its roots by the stream. It does not fear when heat comes; its leaves are always green. It has no worries in a year of drought and never fails to bear fruit.”

Jeremiah 17:7-8 (NIV)

💜 Weekly Encouragement

Total surrender is total trust. Faith is believing when your eyes cannot see. Trust and Faith are both required with God. He rarely shows us His whole plan, but merely one step at a time. We must trust and believe that He will provide and never fail us. Just as this bible verse describes. We must trust and believe that Our leaves will always be green and we will never fail to bear fruit. Although my eyes try to deceive me, I can honestly attest that I have all that I need. Jesus tells us not to worry about tomorrow for today has enough worry of its own. So, Like the tree in this bible verse, I will merely think about today and place my confidence in God.

Blessed is the one who trusts in the Lord

I encourage you to walk boldly with me this week. I will continue to trust and take one step at a time, knowing that God will provide. I will envision being a tree planted by the water this week.

🙏 Prayer

Heavenly Father, thank You for all that you do for me. Thank you for everything that I have. I know that You will continue to provide my needs and I will continue to trust and believe each day-no matter what my eyes see. In the Mighty Name of Jesus Christ, Amen

🌷 Weekly Reflection:
How can you give all your confidence to God this week? How can you trust Him when your eyes do not fully see the picture either? I am going to take one day at a time this week. I will trust Him each day and start over every morning.

I love you no matter what!
🦋 Amber

💜 Remember this week: Our Heavenly Father wants only what is best for us-if He doesn’t do it the way we think-He will do it in a much better way than we ever imagined

You are loved. You are chosen. You are worthy. You are never alone.

Keep saying YES to Jesus...
One step at a time.

🌸 Week of August 3, 2026

Pray about Everything

📖 Scripture

"Do not be anxious about anything, but in every situation, by prayer and petition, with thanksgiving, present your requests to God."

Philippians 4:6 (NIV)

💜 Weekly Encouragement

Instead of worrying, pray about everything. Our Heavenly Father knows our needs and wants to give us what we ask for. Pray and give thanks for all that He has done for you and know that He will also answer your needs as you give them to Him. I can honestly testify that God has provided for me and my needs have been taken care of for nearly an entire year now. It is amazing to see how He works!

Pray about Everything

I encourage you to walk boldly this week and pray about everything. Do not worry. Let your faith and prayers carry you through the week. When you find yourself worrying, stop and pray. Tell your worries and concerns to our Heavenly Father. Let Him show up and show out for you this week! One Yes at a time!

🙏 Prayer

Heavenly Father, thank You for everything that You have done in our lives. Thank you for waking us up again today. You know our needs and our desires. We bring all of our concerns and worries to You, we lay them at Your feet and ask that You handle them for us. Help us keep our eyes on You and Your Kingdom. We give You everything and thank You for all that You have given us. Ensure that Your will be done on Earth as it is in Heaven. We are Your hands and feet on Earth, so please direct us and help us do what You need us to do. In the Mighty Name of Jesus Christ, we pray these things. Amen

🌷 Weekly Reflection:
How can you give all of your worries to God this week? Take time to reflect on what your worries are, how to hand them over and then trust that God will handle them for you. One way I like to do this is write a worry list. I write out my worries, then I crumple the paper up and give them all to God as I throw it away. They are in His hands for Him to handle them in the way He sees fit. It is now my job to trust Him and allow Him to handle them in the way He wants.

Love,
🦋 Amber

💜 Remember this week: Our Heavenly Father loves you! He does not want you to worry about anything. Pray to Him and Worship Him instead of worrying this week. Give your worries to God and do something else instead.

You are loved. You are chosen. You are worthy. You are never alone.

Keep saying YES to Jesus...
One step at a time.

Justice belongs to God

📖 Scripture

“Don’t be misled-you cannot mock the justice of God. You will always harvest what you plant. Those who live only to satisfy their own sinful nature will harvest decay and death from that sinful nature. But those who live to please the Spirit will harvest everlasting life from the Spirit. So let’s not get tire of doing what is good. At just the right time we will reap a harvest of blessing if we don’t give up. Therefore, whenever we have the opportunity, we should do good to everyone-especially to those in the family of faith.

Galatians 6:7-10

💜 Weekly Encouragement

One of the hardest lessons for me as a christian was to give grace to others who have wronged me, but Jesus has been showing me His grace through my healing journey. This verse explains it well -Justice belongs to God. We will always reap what we sow, and so will they. Let us do what is good all the time. God will help us not grow tired of doing what is good. When the time is right, God will ensure we harvest blessings from our good deeds. God will provide justice on our behalf, when He says the time is right. We should all continue to provide others with grace as Jesus has done for us.

Justice belongs to God

When the time is right, justice will be served by God Himself

We should do good to everyone-especially to those in the family of faith.

I encourage you to walk boldly this week with grace, compassion and love. Show everyone the same treatment Jesus has shown you. I guarantee you will not only feel better, but you will help them to feel better too.

🙏 Prayer

Heavenly Father, thank You for always showing me your grace and love. Please help me to provide that same treatment to others. I have made many mistakes and I should certainly forgive others of the same. I know that I am not alone, as You are always with me, providing me with Your Love, Grace and Support. I ask that You help me show others the same. I also ask that when the timing is right for Your judgement, please be gentle with them, for I am certain they know not what they have done. In Jesus' precious name, I pray these words. Amen.

🌷 Weekly Reflection:
Who has wronged you this week-and how can you show them the same Love and Grace that Jesus has bestowed upon you?

Love,

🌸 Week of July 13, 2026

He Goes Before You

📖 Scripture

"The Lord Himself goes before you and will be with you; He will never leave you nor forsake you. Do not be afraid; do not be discouraged."

Deuteronomy 31:8

💜 Weekly Encouragement

One of the greatest comforts we have as believers is knowing that we never walk alone.

Before we take our first step, God has already gone before us.

He already knows the road ahead. He already sees every turn, every blessing, every challenge, and every opportunity waiting for us.

Sometimes we become afraid because we cannot see what tomorrow holds. But God never asked us to trust tomorrow. He asked us to trust Him.

If He goes before us, then we never have to fear the unknown.

Walk confidently this week—not because you know what tomorrow holds, but because you know the One who does.

🙏 Prayer

Heavenly Father, thank You for always going before me. When I cannot see the path ahead, remind me that You already know every step. Replace my fear with faith, my worry with peace, and my uncertainty with confidence in Your promises. Help me trust You more each day and remember that I never walk alone because You are always with me. In Jesus' precious name, Amen.

🌸 July 1, 2026

One Yes Can Change Everything

📖 Scripture

"Trust in the Lord with all your heart and lean not on your own understanding; in all your ways submit to Him, and He will make your paths straight."

Proverbs 3:5–6

💜 Encouragement

Have you ever noticed that God rarely shows us the entire journey?

Instead, He asks for one step. One act of faith. One "yes."

Looking back, I can see that the biggest changes in my life did not happen all at once. They happened each time I trusted God enough to take the next step He placed before me.

That is what being God's Yes Girl means to me. Not being fearless. Not having every answer. Simply believing that His direction is always better than my own.

Maybe today your "yes" looks like forgiving someone. Maybe it is praying when you do not feel like it. Maybe it is beginning something you have been afraid to start.

Whatever it is, remember this: God does not ask us to carry tomorrow. He simply asks us to trust Him today.

One yes can change everything.

🙏 Prayer

Father, teach me to trust You with my whole heart. Help me lay down my plans whenever they compete with Yours. Give me the courage to say yes to whatever You ask, knowing that You are faithful in every step. May my life always point others to You. In Jesus' name, Amen.

Love,
🦋 Amber

💜 Remember today: You are loved. You are chosen. You are worthy. You are never alone.
